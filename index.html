Practice Areas
At Lex & Partners, our expertise spans across multiple legal disciplines:

1. Corporate & Commercial Law

We assist companies in drafting, reviewing, and negotiating commercial agreements, mergers, acquisitions, and compliance issues.

Our goal is to protect your business interests while ensuring long-term growth.

2. Civil & Criminal Litigation

Our litigation team represents clients in civil disputes, contract breaches, personal injury claims, and
criminal defense.

We focus on strong case strategy, thorough research, and persuasive argumentation.

3. Real Estate & Property Law

From property transactions to tenancy disputes, our firm provides reliable legal support to buyers,
sellers, and developers.

4. Family & Divorce Law

We handle sensitive family matters with care, confidentiality, and respect — including divorce, child
custody, and asset division.

5. Employment Law

We guide both employers and employees through workplace disputes, unfair dismissals, and contractual
issues.

6. Intellectual Property (IP) Law

Our IP team protects your creative assets — trademarks, copyrights, and patents — from infringement
and misuse.
